# Circuits behind the authors' plans

Numbers: `results/circuits_map.csv` (one row per circuit name, 332) and `results/p3_networks.csv` (one row per plan
file, 1663), regenerated by `experiments/p3_circuits.py` (about 1 min on one core); checks in `tests/test_circuits.py`.
Code: `qctn.builders` (generators and the QASM paths), `qctn.catalog` (name to source). Release line numbers refer
to `936630a`; QXTools, QXTns and QXZoo are version 1.0.0 as pinned in the release's `Manifest.toml`.

## Result

Each plan is replayed on our network with `qctn.tn.replay` (phase 2): every step's three ranks and the common
indices' qubit tag, prime level and dimension must agree. A plan *passes* when it does and every tensor of the
network is contracted.

## Conventions reproduced

- QXZoo's `GateCall2(symbol, ctrl, target)` (`src/gates/gate_ops.jl` l. 60) stores its first qubit argument as
  `ctrl`; `c_x`, `c_z`, `c_r_phase` (`default_gates.jl` l. 273, 303, 380) and `swap` (`composite_gates.jl` l. 83) pass
  their two qubits through in order, and QXTools' `gate_qubits` (`circuits.jl` l. 83) returns `[target, ctrl]`. So
  `f(a, b)` puts the first half of the split gate on `b`. GHZ's `c_x(i, i+1)` gives `[i+1, i]`.
- `create_qft_circuit` calls `apply_qft!(circ, collect(0:n-1))` (l. 46) and then `c_x(0, 1)`, `c_x(1, 2)`. QXTns indexes
  the qubit wires as a Julia array (`tensor_network_circuit.jl` l. 85), so qubit 0 can't be converted; the plans
  (tags `Qubit1`..`Qubitn`, 4 extra tensors) replay on the same gates on qubits 1..n. The two CX gates aren't part of
  a QFT; they are in every `QFT_<n>` network. The controlled phases' angles go down to 2π/2^n; at n ≤ 37 their
  operator Schmidt rank stays 2 at the SVD threshold 1e-15.
- `QFT_IQFT_<n>`: the release has no code for it. In the `QFT_IQFT_5` plans the gate tensors' wire indices (qubit,
  prime level, in tensor order) give: QFT on qubits 1–3 (t1–t11, the SWAP is t10/t11), then IQFT on 1–5 starting
  with its two SWAPs (t12–t15). With ⌈n/2⌉ the tensor counts fit every n from 5 to 25 and all 126 plans replay.
- RQC: our `rqc` follows `create_RQC` line by line: `patterns` (l. 89), the order `[3, 1, 6, 8, 5, 7, 2, 4]` (l. 171),
  CZ on `lin_idx(col, row, rows)` (l. 179) while H and single-qubit gates use `lin_idx(row, col, cols)` (l. 163,
  188). The two numberings differ (transposed), so a single-qubit gate is placed by the hit pattern of one grid
  qubit and applied to another; this is how the networks were built and we keep it.

## Random circuits and seeds

`create_RQC`'s seed only enters `random_gate!` (`rqc.jl` l. 71–80), which picks the next single-qubit gate on a
qubit as one of the two gates (T, √X, √Y) other than its last. Whether and where a gate is placed depends only on the
pattern, so the network's structure, and every plan replay, is the same for any seed. The plans contain no tensor
values, and the release records no seed, so which of T, √X, √Y the authors drew can't be recovered. Reproducing Julia's
stream would mean reimplementing `MersenneTwister` (dSFMT in Julia 1.9) and `rand(rng, [0, 1])`; even then the seed is
unknown. `rqc(..., seed=...)` draws with numpy instead, following QXZoo's rule (first gate T, no repeat on a qubit).
If the exact values of some run were needed, exporting that circuit once from Julia would be the way; for plan
structure, features and contraction timing the values don't matter. `RANDOM_*` / `Random_IBM_*` come from the MQT
Bench QASM files and are exact.

## Where the plan's name and its network differ

Found by replay; each is listed in `results/circuits_map.csv` (`built_as`, `note`).

- Two QASM readers. The plans of all parseable `Qwalk_<n>` files (11) and the generator-less `QNN_<n>` files (14:
  `QNN_5`, `QNN_10`, `QNN_15*`, `QNN_17`, `QNN_19`, `QNN_20`, `QNN_25*`, `QNN_26_gpu`) replay only on a network built
  without `u1`, `u2`, `p`, `sx` gates and with each two-qubit gate's qubits in the other order. The release's reader
  labels `u2`, `u1`, `p` as "New search patterns" (`funcions_qasm.jl` l. 645, 674, 702) and says it "fixes the bug
  present in the previous version" about control/target order (l. 233–234, 587–588, under "Improvements added on
  15 July 2025", l. 230). The earlier reader isn't in the release; `builders.from_qasm(..., early=True)` is our
  reading of what it did. These networks lack 30–650 single-qubit gates (`QNN`: all `p` and `u2`; `Qwalk`: all `sx`).
  `QWALK_<n>` and the `_pla_` `QNN` plans use the current reader.
- RQC with and without the final Hadamard layer. Non-square names (`RQC_3_7_*`, `RQC_4_5_*`, …, 503 plans) have no
  final layer. Square names (`RQC_<n>_<n>_14`, `RQC_11_11_11`, `RQC_<n>_<d>`) have it, except all seven `RQC_6_20`
  plans and the Jdrasil plan of ten circuits (`RQC_4_32`, `_4_50`, `_4_72`, `_5_16`, `_5_24`, `_5_28`, `_6_16`,
  `_6_24`, `_7_16`, `_7_20`); for `RQC_4_12` and `RQC_4_75` the Jdrasil plan has it too. So within one circuit name,
  the Jdrasil plan was often made on a network with n² fewer tensors than the other six. Phase 2 found that most
  split rows for Jdrasil carry MinFill's features; this may be related, we haven't checked.
- Depth: all five `RQC_3_7_50` plans replay on depth 49 (none on 50); five of seven `RQC_4_6_30` plans replay on
  depth 32, two on depth 30.
- `GROVER_6`: both plans replay on the 5-qubit circuit (`grover-v-chain_..._5`); there is no 6-qubit v-chain file.
- `dj_indep_qiskit_25`: both plans replay on `pricingcall_nativegates_ibm_qiskit_opt3_25` (22,971 tensors).
- `RQC_4_20`, `RQC_4_60` (5 plans, the four-root plans of phase 2): they contract only t1–t193, gate tensors for any
  depth ≥ 17, without input or output tensors. They agree with depth 20 / 60 step by step, but equally with any
  depth from 17 up, so they don't confirm the depth. `RQC_4_11`'s only plan has one step.
